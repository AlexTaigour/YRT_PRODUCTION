01 / What we do

Run it cleanly.

YRT manages esports tournaments from registration through match completion.

The exact scope is agreed before the event: format, admin work, graphics and show responsibilities are confirmed up front.

Registrations

Team sign-ups and slot lists managed clearly.

Match schedules

Rounds and match timing organised for the event.

Lobby operations

Lobby coordination and match administration.

Points tables

Results, standings and leaderboard graphics.

Rules briefing

Participants receive the confirmed format and rules.

Event report

Final outputs and results shared after close.

03 / FAQ

Tournament questions.

Can YRT handle registrations?

Registration scope can be included. Slot limits and team requirements are confirmed with YRT.

Do you manage match disputes?

Dispute handling is a confirmed part of the project scope and must follow the agreed rules.

What format do you support?

Scope is built around the selected game and format, starting with PUBG Mobile.
