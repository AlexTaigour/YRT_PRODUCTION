04 / Jersey production

Your team. Your colours. Your jersey.

Custom team jerseys personalised with player names and roles. Here is how ordering works and what to send us to get started.

Send your team details

DM us your team name, logo and colours. If you do not have a logo yet, our design team can work with your ideas.

Approve the design

We send you a front and back design preview. Production starts only after you approve it.

Production & delivery

Your jerseys are produced to your confirmed size list and delivered to your team.

What to send us to start

  • Team name and logo (or your design idea)
  • Player in-game names and roles
  • Size for every player
  • How many jerseys you need

Made for your team

  • Design preview before production
  • Personalised player names and roles
  • Official merchandise partner of Meowsical Esports
  • Makers of the Wonder Girls official jersey
